---
name: atlas-evaluate
description: Conduct reproducible research-output evaluations, abstention analysis and regression checks
---
Read `methods/EVALUATION.md`, the sealed protocol and its exact `evaluation_criteria`. Preserve held-out cases; never use reference answers to tune prompts, inclusion rules or thresholds. On independently prepared trusted cases, use `python3 -m atlas bench-eval CASES RESULTS`; use retrieval/comparison evaluators only with frozen, rights-cleared corpora and matching keys. The engine requires unique case/result IDs, rejects unknown IDs, and retains missing/failed cases in denominators. Never drop failures, abstentions, blocked runs or incomplete outputs from the accounting.

Report per criterion with its definition, numerator, denominator, sampling/missingness, source access, failures, uncertainty, cost and reproducibility details. Keep factual support, citation faithfulness, decision-dimension coverage, numerical reproduction, source independence, uncertainty, reliability, latency and resource cost separate; do not hide fatal errors in a single composite. Use paired conditions and blinded review when feasible, with matched inputs/resources and predeclared analysis. For calibration, require actual forecasts or probabilities paired with later outcomes and a defined denominator; compute appropriate proper scores/interval coverage and report sample uncertainty. Self-reported model confidence, synthetic fixtures and scenario distributions are not observed calibration.

Distinguish local structural/control tests, synthetic examples, live Copilot sessions, live provider retrieval and independent expert assessments. State what was actually observed, what remains unknown, and any source-selection bias. No small benchmark proves universal superiority or general research quality. Write `evaluation.json` with the protocol's criteria unchanged, actual results, failures, limitations and next actions; set `research_quality_proven` to `false`.
